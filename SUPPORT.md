# Lumi support

For help with the Lumi LED keychain or iPhone app, email kusherbaeveraly@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Lumi app version, and what happened.

## Connecting your keychain

Power on Lumi, keep it near your iPhone, and enable Bluetooth. Allow Bluetooth access when prompted, then select your keychain in the app. If pairing fails, tell us whether Lumi appears in the device list.
