# Lumi privacy policy

For Lumi iOS 0.2.0. Effective November 25, 2026.

## Your iPhone and keychain

Lumi stores your pixel gallery, selected keychain identifier and name, preferences, calibration settings, and saved album colors on your iPhone. Bluetooth transfers artwork and settings to the keychain and reads its status. Links written to an NFC tag can be read by anyone who scans it.

## Music and artwork

Album Glow uses track, album, artist, and artwork information to create colors. Online artwork searches may send artist, album, or track names to Apple’s iTunes Search service and, if needed, Deezer. Lumi downloads artwork from the returned image host. These services receive the request and your network IP address. Lumi caches color grids locally using hashed album or track identifiers.

## Update checks

Lumi contacts GitHub and its file delivery services to check for releases and download updates. These services receive network request information including your IP address.

## Your controls

Manage Bluetooth and media permissions in iPhone Settings. Deleting Lumi removes its local app data from that iPhone. Artwork, settings, and links written to the keychain or tag remain until changed.
